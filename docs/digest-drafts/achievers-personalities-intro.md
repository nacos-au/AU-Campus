Look around you.

The chess player who sees six moves ahead. The one who leads worship on Sunday and counsels people through the week. The speaker who can hold a hall in complete silence. The student quietly paying someone else’s school fees. The barber with a full queue, turning a clipper into a business. The athlete with a brand carrying his name. The musician whose songs you’ve heard through a hostel wall. The content creator with a growing audience. The photographer who sees what the rest of us walk past. The coder shipping software between lectures. The startup founder building something from a dorm room. The writer filling notebooks nobody has read yet. The one whose distinctions haven’t broken in years. The one running at 5 a.m. while the hostels are still asleep. The one whose LinkedIn makes you wonder how you’re on the same campus.

And then the visionaries. The ones who think differently and see what others don’t. The ones with a Goggins mindset, choosing the hard thing again and again, doing extraordinary things that are one in a million.

You’ve probably seen some of them. Maybe you’ve walked past them. Maybe you’ve sat in a lecture with them. Maybe you’ve never met them at all.

Which of them came to mind first? Who do you know that belongs on this list? And who is on it that you’ve never noticed?

You walk past them every day, and it feels normal. It isn’t. If everyday people aren’t doing these things, they’re not normal. Every one of them has a personality and a story worth telling.

There’s an Instagram page I love following. I’ve lived in Nigeria all my life, and this page goes into the homes of peculiar people, interviews them, and shows you the houses. They’re beautiful. You wonder how these homes were in Nigeria all along and you never saw them. Then you meet the people behind them, hear their stories, take in the homes, and it gives you a vision of what’s possible.

We often point at what’s missing. This series points at what’s already here, the things that are present but that we choose to ignore.

Achievers in Plain Sight will interview and introduce the individuals and groups on campus doing uncommon things: their stories, their routines, what they’re building.

Achievers University has achievers. It’s time we met them.
