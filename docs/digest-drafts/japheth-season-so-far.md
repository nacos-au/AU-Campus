Football — what preseason said

Before the league table started filling in, the preseason was meant to give every faculty one match. Nine faculties cannot all be paired off, so four matches were played and eight sides took part. Pharmacy did not play. So this is a first look, not a verdict.

- FOSMAS 2–1 FOLAW
- MLS 1–1 FONAS
- Nursing 3–1 Engineering
- FBMS 1–2 Medicine

The loudest result was Nursing over Engineering. FOSMAS and Medicine also took the win. MLS and FONAS shared the points.

Was there a better way? An odd field always leaves one side out of a single round of pairings, and nine faculties was always going to produce that. The options were a rotating bye, a one-off extra match for the side left out, or a short extra round so everyone played. Each one costs time or a fixture, so none was free.
